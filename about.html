About the Apartment

Raheja Water Front Apartment - A Beautiful Apartment Near Chitrapur Beach, Kulai, Mangalore

Raheja Water Front is a premium apartment complex located near the picturesque Chitrapur Beach in Kulai, Mangalore. This modern apartment offers a serene living experience, with four wings—Pacific A, Pacific B, Atlantic A, and Atlantic B—ensuring ample living space for residents.

The complex features a variety of world-class amenities to enhance the lifestyle of its residents, including:
  • Gym for fitness enthusiasts
  • Swimming pool to relax and unwind
  • Ladies Kitty Parties for socializing and building sense of community
  • Celebrates all festivals and events together
  • Outdoor sports facilities like basketball and badminton courts.
  • Indoor games like UNO, Chess, Table Tennis, and Carom for entertainment and family bonding
  • A playground for children, ensuring they have a safe space to play and explore.
  • A walking track for morning and evening strolls amidst nature.
Raheja Water Front offers both 3 BHK and 2 BHK well-furnished homes, designed for comfort and convenience. The apartments provide breathtaking beach views, making it the perfect place to relax and enjoy the beauty of nature. Whether you're looking for a peaceful environment, recreational facilities, or a family-friendly space, Raheja Water Front is the ideal choice for anyone seeking a high-quality living experience near the beach in Mangalore.
